Description

Lume is a modern and carefully designed studio located in the heart of Athens, near Omonia metro station. It is ideal for short stays, business trips, and travelers who want easy access to the city center. The studio is within walking distance from major attractions such as Monastiraki and the Acropolis, making it a perfect choice for exploring Athens. Fully equipped with WiFi, air conditioning, and self check-in, Lume offers comfort, cleanliness and convenience for couples, solo travelers, and digital nomads.

Why choose Lume?

Lume combines a central location in Athens with modern comfort, making it an excellent choice for visitors who want to stay close to the city's main attractions while enjoying a relaxing space.
