Urban Boho

Modern boho studio in Athens center near Omonia metro and Acropolis.

Photos

Description

Urban Boho is a modern boho-style and carefully designed studio located in the heart of Athens, near Omonia metro station. It is ideal for short stays, business trips, and travelers who want easy access to the city center. The studio is within walking distance from major attractions such as Monastiraki and the Acropolis, making it a perfect choice for exploring Athens. Fully equipped with WiFi, air conditioning, and self check-in, Urban Boho offers comfort, cleanliness and convenience for couples and solo travelers.

Why choose Urban Boho?

Urban Boho combines a central location in Athens with modern comfort, making it an excellent choice for visitors who want to stay close to the city's main attractions while enjoying a relaxing space.
